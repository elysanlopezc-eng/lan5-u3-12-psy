Montano Habit

Intrinsic vs. Extrinsic Motivation (15 minutes)

Core Content

Intrinsic Motivation

Driven by internal rewards, personal interest, and a sense of purpose:

  • Autonomy: Freedom to design campaign creative and manage one's schedule.
  • Mastery: Desire to improve copywriting or data analytics skills.
  • Purpose: Believing in the social or commercial impact of marketing projects.

Extrinsic Motivation

Driven by external tangible or intangible rewards and consequences:

  • Compensation & Bonuses: Sales commissions and performance raises.
  • Recognition: "Marketer of the Month" awards and public praise.
  • Job Security & Status: Promotions and official titles.

Advanced Lexicon: Intrinsic drive, external contingencies, discretionary effort, autonomy-supportive climate, psychological engagement.

• Discuss whether over-relying on cash bonuses can extinguish genuine creative passion in marketing designers.

Motivation Theories & Marketing Team Plan (30 minutes)

Applied Practice

Key Motivation Theories

Maslow’s Hierarchy: Meeting basic physiological/safety needs before higher-level esteem and self-actualization.
Herzberg’s Two-Factor Theory: Separating hygiene factors (salary, policy) from motivators (achievement, growth).
Self-Determination Theory (SDT): Autonomy, competence, and relatedness as core psychological needs.

Team Activity: Marketing Engagement Plan

In teams, design a 4-pillar motivation strategy for a tired marketing agency team:

  • Pillar 1 (Autonomy): Flexible remote/hybrid working hours.
  • Pillar 2 (Recognition): Peer-to-peer shoutouts and creative awards.
  • Pillar 3 (Growth): Sponsored UX/UI design workshops.
  • Pillar 4 (Purpose): Linking campaigns to social impact missions.
Montano Habit: Begin with the End in Mind (Designing a culture where engagement fuels long-term retention).
